Frequently Asked Questions

We operate Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. Emergency support is available 24/7 for urgent matters. You can reach us via email anytime, and we'll respond within 24 hours.

You can track your booking status directly from your dashboard. Log in to your account, navigate to "My Bookings," and you'll see real-time updates of your service appointments and caregiver assignments.

Cancellations made 48 hours before the scheduled service are free. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a service fee. Emergency cancellations are handled on a case-by-case basis. Please contact our support team for specific situations.

You can update your account information anytime through your profile settings. Click on "My Profile" in the sidebar, then select "Edit Profile" to modify your personal information, contact details, and preferences.

Currently, we operate within the United States only. However, we're expanding our services to other regions. Contact our support team to be notified when we launch services in your area.

View all FAQs for more information. If you still can't find an answer, please contact our support team directly via email, phone, or live chat.
